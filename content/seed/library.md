# Library Services

## Timings

The central library is open from **08:00 to 22:00** on weekdays and from **10:00 to 17:00** on
Saturdays. It remains closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow **four books** at a time for **fourteen days**. A renewal of
seven days is allowed if the book is not reserved by another reader. Reference volumes and
journals are for reading in the library only.

## Fines

An overdue fine of **Rs 5 per book per day** applies. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at
the current market price plus a processing fee of Rs 200.

## Digital Resources

The library subscribes to IEEE Xplore, Springer and JSTOR. Access is available on campus over
the campus network. Off campus access requires a VPN account from the IT helpdesk.

## Study Rooms

Six group study rooms are available on the first floor. Bookings are made at the circulation
desk for a maximum of two hours per group per day.
