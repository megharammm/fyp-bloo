# Hostel Rules and Facilities

## Quiet Hours

Quiet hours run from **22:00 to 06:00** every day. Loud music and group study in the corridors
are not permitted during these hours.

## Visitors

Day visitors must register at the gate and leave by **21:00**. Overnight guests are not
permitted in student rooms. Violations are reported to the warden.

## Mess Timings

Breakfast is served from **07:30 to 09:30**, lunch from **12:30 to 14:00**, and dinner from
**19:30 to 21:00**. A late plate can be requested from the mess office until 21:30.

## Room Allotment

First year students are allotted shared rooms in Block A and Block B. Room changes are allowed
only at the end of a semester and require warden approval.

## Maintenance

Report electrical or plumbing faults through the maintenance desk on the ground floor. Urgent
issues are attended within four hours; routine requests within two working days.

## Warden Contact

The chief warden is available in the hostel office from 09:00 to 17:00, or by email at
warden@bloo.edu.
