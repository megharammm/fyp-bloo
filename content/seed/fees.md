# Fees and Payments

## Tuition Fee 2026-27

The tuition fee for the B.Tech programme is **Rs 1,20,000 per year**, payable in two
installments of Rs 60,000 before 15 July and 15 December. A late payment fine of Rs 500 per
week applies after the due date.

## Hostel Fee

The hostel fee is **Rs 48,000 per semester**, payable before 15 August 2026. Mess charges are
billed separately at **Rs 3,200 per month**.

## Refund Policy

Refunds are pro-rata and require a written application to the hostel warden within 30 days of
vacating the room. The security deposit of Rs 5,000 is refunded in full after room inspection.
Tuition fees are non-refundable after the second week of the semester.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships cover 50 percent of tuition for students with a CGPA above 8.5. Applications
close on **30 September 2026** at the administrative office.

## Contact

For fee queries, contact the accounts office at accounts@bloo.edu or visit room 104 in the
administrative block between 10:00 and 16:00 on weekdays.
