# Transport and Bus Routes

## Campus Shuttle

The campus shuttle runs every **20 minutes** from 07:00 to 21:00 between the main gate, the
academic block and the hostels.

## City Bus Routes

- **Route 4** serves the north campus and the railway station, departing every 30 minutes.
- **Route 12** connects the south campus with the city market.
- **Route 19** runs to the airport twice a day, at 06:00 and 18:00.

## Bus Pass

A semester bus pass costs **Rs 1,800** for students and is issued at the transport office.
Applications require a student ID card and a passport photograph.

## Parking

Two-wheeler parking is available behind the academic block. Cars require a permit from the
security office. Parking inside the academic quadrangle is prohibited.

## Lost Property

Items left on campus buses are handed to the transport office within 24 hours and held for
fourteen days.
