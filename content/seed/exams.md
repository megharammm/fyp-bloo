# Examinations and Results

## End Semester Examinations

End semester examinations for the autumn term begin on **20 November 2026** and run until
5 December 2026. The detailed timetable is published on the examination notice board two weeks
before the start date.

## Hall Tickets

Hall tickets are released on **10 November 2026**. Students with attendance below 75 percent
will not receive a hall ticket unless a medical exemption has been approved by the dean.

## Revaluation

Applications for revaluation open **three days after the result announcement** and close after
ten days. The fee is **Rs 500 per paper**. Results of revaluation are published within four
weeks.

## Backlog Examinations

Backlog examinations are held in the last week of January and the last week of July. Students
must register at least fifteen days in advance through the examination portal.

## Malpractice

Any candidate found using unauthorised material is debarred from the current examination and
the next two examinations. Appeals must be filed within seven days to the examination committee.
